Ecological Management & Restoration – Call for New Editors
Ecological Management & Restoration, one of two journals published by the Ecological Society of Australia and Wiley Publishing, is seeking to expand the membership of its Editorial Board.
The topics on which the Journal receives submissions has broadened over the last few years, and the number of people accessing the Journal has increased. To keep apace with our evolving context, we’re looking to recruit new Editors. Specifically, we are seeking individuals with experience and expertise in the following topics:
Indigenous land and sea management
Coral reef ecology and management
Estuarine ecology and management
Urban ecology
Zoology, especially of invasive species
Aquatic biology and ecology, especially of freshwater fish
Social psychology and conservation psychology
Behavioural economics
In particular, we are seeking land managers with an interest in conservation and restoration, as well as staff from government agencies and non-government organisations with experience and expertise in land management.
Successful candidates will have: 
at least five years of professional work experience in planning, management and/or research in natural resource management;
strong written communication skills; and
the capacity to oversee the assessment of up to three manuscripts a year.
Experience in writing peer-reviewed publications is preferred but not essential.
Having a PhD is not a prerequisite for being a member of the Editorial Board.
Ideally you will also already be a member of the ESA.
How to apply:
To express and interest in taking-up a role on the Editorial Board, please email a short CV to EMR Editor Andrew Knight (*protected email*) ahead of meeting in-person or online.
