Abstract
Residential outdoor landscapes constitute the most frequently encountered visual environment for urban residents, yet systematic evaluation of their aesthetic quality and ecological service value remains lacking. This study developed an integrated three-stage framework combining deep learning semantic segmentation, the Scenic Beauty Estimation method, and machine learning regression modeling to assess public visual aesthetic preference for residential outdoor landscapes, understood as one measurable component of the cultural ecosystem services provided by urban residential green spaces. A PSPNet model performed pixel-level semantic segmentation on 2816 field-collected landscape images from 32 residential communities in central Chengdu (mIoU = 0.763), extracting 13 element categories to construct a residential landscape feature indicator system. Public aesthetic preference scores were obtained through 276 valid questionnaires. Pearson correlation analysis identified the Green Visual Index (GVI) as the strongest positive predictor (r = 0.57), while the Interference Factor Index (IFI) showed the most pronounced negative correlation (r = − 0.46). The random forest model achieved R2 = 0.796, substantially outperforming the stepwise OLS regression (adjusted R2 = 0.517). SHAP interpretability analysis revealed a marginal contribution saturation effect for GVI at approximately 35–40%, an inverted U-shaped threshold for Building Visual Index at approximately 30%, and accelerating negative effects when IFI exceeded 5%. These nonlinear thresholds represent exploratory, model-derived perceptual benchmarks that can inform the optimization of residential green space configuration, pending further validation with ecological performance data. Residential landscapes were classified into three aesthetic preference levels with differentiated improvement strategies proposed, offering data-driven decision support for urban green infrastructure planning and the enhancement of visual aesthetic quality in residential environments.
