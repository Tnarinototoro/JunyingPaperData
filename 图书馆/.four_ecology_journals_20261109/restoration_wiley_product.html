Description
Restoration Ecology facilitates interdisciplinary advances in ecological restoration from regional to global scales. We are paving the way to a crucial shift in restoration ecology, by publishing original papers on the full range of experimental, theoretical, technical, practical, policy, governance, and strategic research and applications across all terrestrial and aquatic ecosystems and taxa.  Restoration Ecology is an internationally recognized journal of the Society for Ecological Restoration, publishing research contributing to restoring damaged ecosystems.
