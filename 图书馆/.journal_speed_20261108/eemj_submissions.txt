Submissions




Before submitting


    Please make sure that you have carefully read the Instructions for authors, as well as the notice on scientific ethics. See also the sample manuscript, which constitutes an example on what a proper submission should look like.


Submission Preparation Checklist

As part of the submission process, authors are required to check off their submission's compliance with all of the following items, and submissions may be returned to authors that do not adhere to these guidelines.

	
The submission has not been previously published, nor has it been submitted to another journal / conference / event for consideration.

The submission file is in Microsoft Word .doc format (NOT .docx).

The manuscript has been elaborated according to the guidelines found in the Instructions for authors.

The manuscript does not contain elements of plagiarism or self plagiarism and conforms with the norms of ethics in publishing.

All authors have given their consent for the submission of the manuscript.
