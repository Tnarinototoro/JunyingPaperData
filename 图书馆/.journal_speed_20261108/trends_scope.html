Aims & Scope

Trends in Sciences (TiS) is a peer-reviewed journal covering all areas of science and technology, launched in 2021. It is published 12 Issues (Monthly) by Scholarly Publishing and Repository Integration Center (SPRINT). The scope of the journal includes the following areas of research:

Biological Sciences and Medicine
Physical Sciences
Applied Sciences

Manuscripts should be written in English and be submitted online at https://tis.wu.ac.th.
