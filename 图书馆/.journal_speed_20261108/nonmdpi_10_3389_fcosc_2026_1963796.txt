Conserv. Sci. 7:1896568. doi: 10.3389/fcosc.2026.1896568 In the published article, the contribution of ANP was inadvertently omitted from the Acknowledgments section. The corrected statement is: “This study includes data generated to fulfil environmental licensing requirements established by the Brazilian Institute of Environment and Renewable Natural Resources (IBAMA). Part of the dataset was obtained through the Scientific Passive Acoustic Monitoring Project (PMAPC), under IBAMA administrative process no. 02001.031701/2023-19. The authors thank TGS, Shearwater, and Viridien for supporting the PMAPC and PCMC activities that contributed data to this study. We also acknowledge Mineral Engenharia e Meio Ambiente for implementing the PMID project in accordance with IBAMA requirements under administrative process no. 02022.000336/2014-53. The data for 2026 are part of the MAPC project, funded by Petrobras (Brazil) through the ANP (Agência Nacional do Petróleo) Research, Development and Innovation (R&D&I) Clause (PT-200.20.00248). We also thank AUSET, particularly Gustavo Miranda, for support with acoustic equipment and valuable technical assistance, as well as the field assistants who contributed to data collection across all projects.” The original version of this article has been updated.
