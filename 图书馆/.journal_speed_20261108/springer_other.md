## 官网原句与筛选影响

- Discover Cities：`supports multidisciplinary research and policy developments across the field of urban science`。其Topics明确含 **Urban Planning, Design and Transport**、**Urban Agriculture and Forestry**、**Urban Health and Well-being**。用户名单这三项在该官网是主题类别，不能当作三本已核实的独立期刊。可统一归到Discover Cities选刊行。
- Urban Ecosystems：`relationships between socioeconomic and ecological structures and processes in urban environments`；范围含 `policy`、`social and human ecology`、`urban planning`。并有Ecology with Cities的短稿类型，涉及与社会协作来研究/管理城市社会生态系统；如走短稿需要按类型改写，不能把现有全文直接当同一稿件类型。
- Environmental Management：`publishes research on the assessment, use, conservation, and management of the environment and natural resources`；`Case study research is also appropriate, provided that the results and lessons emerging are of relevance to an international audience.` 原创研究通常10,000词以内含参考文献。
- Landscape Ecology：`studies are characterized by spatially explicit methods in which spatial attributes and arrangements of landscape elements are directly analyzed and related to ecological processes`。这是现稿核心缺口，10天首决不能弥补scope不合。
- Discover Environment：范围含 `Ecology, biodiversity`、`Conservation and restoration of degraded ecosystems`、`Urban environmental problems`、`Innovation strategies, policies, and economics in the environmental sector`。
- Discover Sustainability：范围含 `biodiversity conservation`、`cities and urbanisation`、`environmental education and behaviour`、`governance and regulation`、`natural resources management`。还要求面向真实问题、实施与改变的贡献。
- Environmental Conservation：[作者说明](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/environmental-conservation/information/author-instructions)写：`Selected manuscripts are sent out to external reviewers, who agree to undertake the review within 30 days.` **此30天是审稿人承诺的审稿期限，不是投稿至首决或接收的全流程统计。** 还写需 `Natural environment(s), ecosystem(s) or species conservation goal`、`international readership targeted`；Research Paper限6000词、6个图表，Report限4000词、4个图表。其[metrics页](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/environmental-conservation/information/about-this-journal/journal-metrics)本次没有全流程处理时间；[review process页](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/environmental-conservation/information/peer-review-information/review-process)说明single-anonymized。
- URBAN DESIGN International：scope写不收简单方法重复，除非产生新知识；并明确 `we cannot accept papers derived largely from dissertations, theses, conference proceedings, or any previously published articles.` 这与用户是否学位论文稿相关，不能擅自认定符合或不符合，但应如实提示该刊明确规则。
- Environmental Earth Sciences：[scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/12665/aims-and-scope)明确2026-01-01转为Fully OA，2025-08-19后所收稿接受出版时涉及APC。Landscape Ecology官网亦标Open access，不能自动按学校Springer hybrid名额承诺覆盖。
