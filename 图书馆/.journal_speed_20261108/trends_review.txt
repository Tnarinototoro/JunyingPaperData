Peer Review Process
            
            
            
          
Process Insights: Peer Review and Production Workflow
            
              
                
1
                
                  
Submission
                  
The author submits the manuscript to the journal through the submission system (OJS platform).
                    
The manuscript enters the initial assessment phase conducted by the author.
                  
              
              
                
2
                
                  
Editorial Check  1 - 3 Day
                  
The Editor-in-Chief or editorial team conducts an initial evaluation within 1-3 day to assess:
                        
Relevance of the manuscript to the journal’s scope.
                        
Compliance with the journal's policies and formatting requirements.
                        
Preliminary evaluation of the manuscript’s quality.
                      
                  
                    Outcome:
                      
If unsuitable, manuscript may be rejected at this stage.
                      
If it meets criteria, it proceeds to the peer review phase.
                    
                
              
              
                
3
                
                  
Peer Review  4 - 6 Weeks
                  
Qualified peer reviewers evaluate the manuscript in 4-6 weeks, considering:
                        
Originality and contribution to the field.
                        
Scientific validity and methodology.
                        
Technical accuracy.
                        
Suitability of references.
                        
Clarity and conciseness.
                        
Ethical compliance and overall quality.
                      
                  
                    Possible Outcomes:
                      
Reject: Manuscript is declined.
                      
Accept: Manuscript accepted without revisions.
                      
Revision Required: Authors revise and resubmit for further review.
                    
                
              
              
                
4
                
                  
Editorial Decision  1 - 2 Week
                  
After receiving reviewers' feedback, the editorial team makes a final decision within 1-2 week.
                  
                    Possible Editorial Decisions:
                      
Accept: Manuscript proceeds to production.
                      
Reject: Manuscript is declined.
                      
Revise and Resubmit: Authors revise and resubmit for re-evaluation.
                    
                
              
              
                
5
                
                  
Invoicing
                  
Upon acceptance, authors receive an Invoice for the Article Processing Charges (APC).
                  
              
              
                
6
                
                  
Copy Editing  3 - 4 Week
                  
The manuscript undergoes typesetting and formatting to ensure:
                        
Consistency with the journal’s style.
                        
Correction of grammatical, typographical, and formatting errors.
                        
Proper citation and referencing style.
                      
                  
              
              
                
7
                
                  
Proofreading  5 - 7 Days
                  
The edited manuscript is sent to the author for proofreading and final approval.
                  
              
              
                
8
                
                  
Plagiarism Check  1 - 3 Day
                  
A plagiarism check is conducted to ensure originality using plagiarism detection software.
                  
              
              
                
9
                
                  
Assigned DOI Crossref
                  
A DOI is assigned and registered with Crossref for citation and referencing.
                  
              
              
                
10
                
                  
Publication
                  
After completing all checks, the manuscript is published on the journal's platform.
                    
Authors are notified of the publication.
