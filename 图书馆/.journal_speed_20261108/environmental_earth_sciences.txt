It focuses on the study of anthropogenically altered interactions within the geosphere and between the geosphere–biosphere. It strives to provide unbiased scientific evidence for sustainable management of natural resources and mitigation of geological, chemical and biological hazards, drawn on academic and industrial expertise in geophysics, geochemistry, biogeosciences, engineering geology, hydrogeology, and physical geography. It has become an open access journal on 1 January 2026. Please see the FAQs at the bottom of this page for more information on funding options, including a limited number of waivers. Nearly all (96%) of surveyed authors expressed willingness to publish again.
