You can register here Notify me Abstract This article describes the nomenclature and classification of insects and other invertebrates by the Tariana people and provides an overview of their cultural uses as sources of food and indicators of climatic and agricultural cycles. The paper also explores the role of these "small animals" in mythology, cosmology and contemporary shamanistic practices including protective blessings and virulent curses. Based on interviews with Tariana knowledge keepers and the primary author's expertise in scientific entomology, the article explores how such cultural practices are deeply informed by taxonomic, morphological, ecological and behavioral characteristics of different invertebrate species.
