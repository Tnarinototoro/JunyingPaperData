You can register here Notify me Abstract The present review intends to offer a thorough and methodical examination of the current literature, which has been conspicuously lacking in spite of the increasing awareness of the multipurpose functions of home and domestic gardens. This review conducts a thorough two-stage analysis to fill the gap in published research on tree species for home and domestic gardens. A total 486 pertinent articles were found during the first stage, which involved a bibliometric analysis of the Scopus and Web of Science databases from 1978 to 2024. According to this analysis, publications have been steadily rising since 2015, reaching a peak in 2021. With the greatest number of authors from the USA, India, and Ethiopia, important research areas include ecology, agriculture, and environmental sciences, with particular attention to biodiversity, sustainable gardening, and garden management. Common species and their contributions to ecosystem services were among the four thematic areas that comprised the second stage, which involved a qualitative review of the identified articles. The review identified a diverse range of tree species associated with home and domestic gardens across different geographical regions, highlighting their contributions to food production, biodiversity, shade, household resources, and other ecosystem services.With a high concentration in tropical and subtropical areas, the review found 61 tree species from a wide geographic range. The results emphasize that homes and domestic gardens are multipurpose systems that contribute to biodiversity conservation, food security, sustainable resource use, and effective garden managementoffer important ecological and socioeconomic services, despite their differences in rural or urban settings.
