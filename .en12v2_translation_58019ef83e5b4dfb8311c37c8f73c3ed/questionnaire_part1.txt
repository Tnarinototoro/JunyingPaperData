Questionnaire survey on conflicts between nature restoration and recreational use in parks and green spaces

This survey asks park management organizations and government officials about their views on the management and operation of urban parks and other green spaces where nature restoration is undertaken, and about measures and practical approaches to balancing nature restoration and recreational use.

We would appreciate a response from someone familiar with the actual conditions of facility development and management and natural environment conservation in the park (埼玉県生態系保護協会; Saitama Prefecture Ecosystem Conservation Association).

The questions concern the effects of various forms of park use by the general public on nature restoration activities undertaken within the park. In this questionnaire, nature restoration and recreational use are defined as follows:

* Nature restoration: activities undertaken within the park to conserve and restore the habitats of diverse organisms.
* Recreational use: park users' use of the various facilities within the park for recreation and other activities.

The questionnaire comprises 12 pages, including the cover and question pages.
