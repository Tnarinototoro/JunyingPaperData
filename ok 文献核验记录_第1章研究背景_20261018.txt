文献核验记录（2026-10-18）

核验范围：用于第1章「（1）研究の背景」的英文文献。核验方式：Crossref DOI 元数据、OpenAlex 元数据/摘要及 DOI 重定向页面。当前记录不等同于逐篇阅读全文；只有在摘要或题名能够直接支持论断时，才将文献用于相应论断。

一、可直接用于背景部分的已核验文献

1. Gobster, P. H. (2001). Visions of nature: Conflict and compatibility in urban park restoration. Landscape and Urban Planning, 56(1–2), 35–51. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0169-2046(01)00164-5
   用途：城市公园自然恢复中，不同自然观、恢复目标与公园利用之间的冲突与兼容。

2. Chiesura, A. (2004). The role of urban parks for the sustainable city. Landscape and Urban Planning, 68(1), 129–138. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.landurbplan.2003.08.003
   用途：城市公园的生态、社会、休闲和福祉等多重功能。

3. Andersson, E., Barthel, S., Borgström, S., Colding, J., Elmqvist, T., Folke, C., & Gren, Å. (2014). Reconnecting cities to the biosphere: Stewardship of green infrastructure and urban ecosystem services. Ambio, 43(4), 445–453. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13280-014-0506-y
   用途：城市绿地作为社会—生态系统，地方管理者/守护者、跨边界管理和治理的重要性。Crossref和OpenAlex摘要均直接支持治理与管理论点。

4. Reed, M. S. (2008). Stakeholder participation for environmental management: A literature review. Biological Conservation, 141(10), 2417–2431. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2008.07.014
   用途：环境管理中的利益相关者参与、知识整合和决策过程。适合支撑治理段落，但不是城市公园专门研究。

5. Aronson, M. F. J., Lepczyk, C. A., Evans, K. L., Goddard, M. A., Lerman, S. B., MacIvor, J. S., Nilon, C. H., & Vargo, T. (2017). Biodiversity in the city: Key challenges for urban green space management. Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment, 15(4), 189–196. https://doi.org/10.1002/fee.1480
   用途：城市绿地生物多样性管理受治理、经济、社会网络、多主体、偏好和社会约束等因素共同影响；需要平衡人类需求与生态过程。OpenAlex摘要直接支持这些论点。

6. Ives, C. D., Lentini, P. E., Threlfall, C. G., Ikin, K., Shanahan, D. F., Garrard, G. E., Bekessy, S. A., Fuller, R. A., Mumaw, L., Rayner, L., Rowe, R., Valentine, L. E., & Kendal, D. (2016). Cities are hotspots for threatened species. Global Ecology and Biogeography, 25(1), 117–126. https://doi.org/10.1111/geb.12404
   用途：城市具有受威胁物种保护价值。注意：研究对象为澳大利亚城市，不宜直接泛化为所有城市公园。

7. Nielsen, A. B., van den Bosch, M., Maruthaveeran, S., & van den Bosch, C. K. (2014). Species richness in urban parks and its drivers: A review of empirical evidence. Urban Ecosystems, 17(1), 305–327. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11252-013-0316-1
   用途：城市公园物种丰富度及其驱动因素。适合支撑公园环境条件与生物多样性之间的背景联系。

8. Lepczyk, C. A., Aronson, M. F. J., Evans, K. L., Goddard, M. A., Lerman, S. B., & MacIvor, J. S. (2017). Biodiversity in the city: Fundamental questions for understanding the ecology of urban green spaces for biodiversity conservation. BioScience, 67(9), 799–807. https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix079
   用途：城市绿地生物多样性研究与管理的核心问题。可作为Aronson et al. (2017)的生态学补充。

9. Monz, C. A., Pickering, C. M., & Hadwen, W. L. (2013). Recent advances in recreation ecology and the implications of different relationships between recreation use and ecological impacts. Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment, 11(8), 441–446. https://doi.org/10.1890/120358
   用途：休闲活动与生态影响之间并非单一线性关系，管理方式需要结合生态响应和利用强度。此前记录中只写“Monz et al., 2013”，应补全为该条目。

10. Larson, C. L., Reed, S. E., Merenlender, A. M., & Crooks, K. R. (2016). Effects of recreation on animals revealed as widespread through a global systematic review. PLOS ONE, 11(12), e0167259. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0167259
    用途：全球系统综述显示休闲活动对动物的影响广泛存在；适合支撑利用活动可能产生生态负荷。OpenAlex摘要显示，274篇文献中超过93%报告至少一种影响，59%被归为负面影响。

11. Pickering, C. M., & Hill, W. (2007). Impacts of recreation and tourism on plant biodiversity and vegetation in protected areas in Australia. Journal of Environmental Management, 85(4), 791–800. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2006.11.021
    用途：休闲和旅游对植物多样性及植被的影响。可用于植物/植被管理的具体论述，但年份较早。

12. Soga, M., & Gaston, K. J. (2016). Extinction of experience: The loss of human–nature interactions. Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment, 14(2), 94–101. https://doi.org/10.1002/fee.1225
    用途：人们日常接触自然的减少及其对环境态度和行为的影响。适合支撑城市公园提供日常自然接触机会。

13. Soga, M., Gaston, K. J., Koyanagi, T. F., Kurisu, K., & Hanaki, K. (2016). Urban residents’ perceptions of neighbourhood nature: Does the extinction of experience matter? Biological Conservation, 203, 143–150. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2016.09.020
    用途：城市居民邻里自然感知与“自然体验消失”之间的关系。若第1章强调城市居民的日常自然接触，优先使用此条或与Soga & Gaston (2016)综述并用。

13. Buchel, S., & Frantzeskaki, N. (2015). Citizens’ voice: A case study about perceived ecosystem services by urban park users in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Ecosystem Services, 12, 169–177. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecoser.2014.11.014
    用途：城市公园使用者感知的生态系统服务和公众价值；适合支撑公园的社会功能与使用者需求。

14. Arnberger, A., & Eder, R. (2015). Are urban visitors’ general preferences for green-spaces similar to their preferences when seeking stress relief? Urban Forestry & Urban Greening, 14(4), 872–882. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ufug.2015.07.005
    用途：城市绿地访问者偏好与压力缓解需求；适合使用者功能段落，但不直接支撑生物多样性冲突。

二、可用于后续「既存研究と本研究の位置づけ」的已核验文献

15. López-Mosquera, N., & Sánchez, M. (2012). Theory of Planned Behavior and the Value-Belief-Norm Theory explaining willingness to pay for a suburban park. Journal of Environmental Management, 113, 251–262. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2012.08.029

16. López-Mosquera, N., García, T., & Barrena, R. (2014). An extension of the Theory of Planned Behavior to predict willingness to pay for the conservation of an urban park. Journal of Environmental Management, 135, 91–99. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2014.01.019

17. Huang, Y., Aguilar, F., Yang, J., Qin, Y., & Wen, Y. (2021). Predicting citizens’ participatory behavior in urban green space governance: Application of the extended theory of planned behavior. Urban Forestry & Urban Greening, 61, 127110. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ufug.2021.127110

18. Maleksaeidi, H., & Keshavarz, M. (2019). What influences farmers’ intentions to conserve on-farm biodiversity? An application of the theory of planned behavior in Fars Province, Iran. Global Ecology and Conservation, 20, e00698. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gecco.2019.e00698
    OpenAlex摘要确认：以274名农户为样本，扩展TPB加入知识、道德规范和农业集约化威胁知觉；态度、威胁知觉、知识、社会规范和道德规范是意图的重要解释因素。

19. Tama, R. A. Z., Ying, L., Yu, M., Hoque, M. M., Adnan, K. M., & Sarker, S. A. (2021). Assessing farmers’ intention towards conservation agriculture by using the Extended Theory of Planned Behavior. Journal of Environmental Management, 280, 111654. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2020.111654

20. Koskela, T., & Karppinen, H. (2024). Forest Owners’ Intention to Safeguard Forest Biodiversity: An Application of the Theory of Planned Behavior. Forest Science, 70(1), 64–79. https://doi.org/10.1093/forsci/fxad044
    注意：此前记录写为Koskela & Karppinen (2023)，Crossref正式卷期为2024年；DOI元数据年份为2023年在线发表。论文正文若按卷期参考文献表，建议写2024；若期刊格式采用online first，可按目标期刊规则处理。

21. Nunez Godoy, C. C., Pienaar, E. F., & Branch, L. C. (2022). Willingness of private landowners to participate in forest conservation in the Chaco region of Argentina. Forest Policy and Economics, 138, 102708. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.forpol.2022.102708

22. Tokunaga, K., Sugino, H., Nomura, H., & Michida, Y. (2020). Norms and the willingness to pay for coastal ecosystem restoration: A case of the Tokyo Bay intertidal flats. Ecological Economics, 169, 106423. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolecon.2019.106423

23. Yuriev, A., Dahmen, M., Paillé, P., Boiral, O., & Guillaumie, L. (2020). Pro-environmental behaviors through the lens of the theory of planned behavior: A scoping review. Resources, Conservation and Recycling, 155, 104660. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.resconrec.2019.104660

24. Klöckner, C. A. (2013). A comprehensive model of the psychology of environmental behaviour—A meta-analysis. Global Environmental Change, 23(5), 1028–1038. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gloenvcha.2013.05.014

25. Steg, L., & Vlek, C. (2009). Encouraging pro-environmental behaviour: An integrative review and research agenda. Journal of Environmental Psychology, 29(3), 309–317. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvp.2008.10.004

26. Brocki, M., Christoffersen, L., Randrup, T. B., Emilsson, T., Sjöman, H. D., & Jansson, M. (2025). Green space management for biodiversity: Swedish Managers’ experience of implementation and influence. Urban Forestry & Urban Greening, 113, 129126. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ufug.2025.129126
    OpenAlex摘要确认：33名绿地管理者参与研讨会；政治和治理优先级、组织内部优先级、社会对自然的总体优先级、经济资源、法律法规和组织内外沟通是重要影响因素。该文是质性/研讨会研究，不能称为TPB或SEM研究。

27. Maleknia, R. (2026). One theory, many outcomes: A systematic review of the theory of planned behavior in forest conservation contexts. Environmental Challenges, 24, 101612. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envc.2026.101612
    OpenAlex摘要确认：综述森林相关情境中的TPB研究，指出态度、主观规范和知觉行为控制的影响存在情境差异，扩展TPB通常提高解释力但缺乏理论整合。可用于说明不能机械照搬TPB；不应写成“专门综述城市公园管理者”。

28. Bodin, Ö. (2017). Collaborative environmental governance: Achieving collective action in social-ecological systems. Science, 357(6352), eaan1114. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan1114
    OpenAlex摘要确认：利益相关者数量多、政治边界与生物物理过程不一致、社会生态系统复杂时，协作网络及其参与者关系会影响集体行动能力。

29. Newig, J., & Fritsch, O. (2009). Environmental governance: Participatory, multi-level—and effective? Environmental Policy and Governance, 19(3), 197–214. https://doi.org/10.1002/eet.509
    用途：参与式、多层次环境治理的有效性问题；适合文献综述中的治理理论，不必放入研究背景第一段。

三、已发现的修正事项

1. “Monz et al. (2013)”应具体写为Monz, Pickering, & Hadwen (2013)，DOI为10.1890/120358，期刊为Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment, 11(8), 441–446。
2. Koskela & Karppinen的DOI对应Forest Science 70(1), 64–79，正式卷期年份为2024；此前写2023是online-first年份，需按投稿期刊格式统一。
3. Fenitra等人的条目真实存在：Fenitra, R. M., Laila, N., Premananto, G. C., Abbas, A., & Sedera, R. M. H. (2023). Explaining littering prevention among park visitors using the Theory of Planned Behavior and Norm Activation Model. International Journal of Geoheritage and Parks, 11(1), 39–53. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijgeop.2022.11.002。它适合“游客环境行为”文献，不适合直接支撑“公园管理者行为”空白。
4. “Aronson et al. (2017)”和“Lepczyk et al. (2017)”是两篇不同文章，均真实存在，不能混用：前者偏城市绿地管理挑战，后者偏城市绿地生物多样性生态学问题。
5. Ives et al. (2016) Crossref正式年份为2016；其研究结论基于澳大利亚城市，应避免无条件泛化至日本都市公园。
6. 第1章背景部分推荐引用Chiesura (2004)、Aronson et al. (2017)、Gobster (2001)、Andersson et al. (2014)、Bodin (2017)、Monz et al. (2013)、Larson et al. (2016)、Soga & Gaston (2016)或Soga et al. (2016)。TPB应用文献主要放到「既存研究と本研究の位置づけ」，避免背景段落理论负担过重。
