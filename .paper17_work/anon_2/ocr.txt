Earth's well-being depends mainly on the conservation Of ecosystem goods and services.
However ， biodiversity is extensively threatened in agricultural landscapes. Since farmers'

vation, this study was conducted tO clarify the underlying constructs Of farmers' intention
tO preserve biodiversity. The Theory Of Planned Behavior was applied tO investigate the
intention Of a sample Of 274 lranian farmers towards on-farm biodiversity conservation. ln
order tO increase the predictive power Of this theory, the new constructs Of knowledge,
moral norm and perceived threats Of intensified agriculture were added tO the model. The
results indicated that farmers have a moderate level 0f knowledge about the value and
strategies Of biodiversity conservation. AISO, they have a favorable attitude toward pre-
serving biodiversity. However, they dO not perceive enough control tO engage in biodi-
versity conservauon practices. Furthermore, the findings revealed that attitude, perceived
threats Of intensified agriculture, knowledge, social norms and moral norms are the most
relevant factors explaining the farmers' intentions toward biodiversity conservation. ln
general the results presented evidence about effectiveness Of the Theory Of Planned
Behavior and the extended model Of planned behavior on assessing the farmers' intention

Accordingly, a number Of considerations about improving farmers' intention tO preserve
