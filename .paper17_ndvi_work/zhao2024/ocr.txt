6 ． Conclusion
Urban green spaces represent a highly accessible form of green
infrastructure which have stlbstantial welfare benefits. Most sttldies in
the field 0f green ptlblic space have considered users ， interaction with
green in urban parks and neighborhood open spaces as separate research
