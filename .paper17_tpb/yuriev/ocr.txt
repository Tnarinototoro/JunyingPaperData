The theory 0f planned behavior (TPB) allows researchers t0 identify the determinants 0f environmental behavior

2 · An overview Of the strengths and limitations Of the TPB
The success Of the TPB can be explained on the basis Of three
strengths in its framework. First, scholars using the TPB can not only

importance Of each belief. Such analyses usually contain three ele-
